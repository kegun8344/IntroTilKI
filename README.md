# Movie Night Matcher

**Stop scrolling, start watching.** 2 to 6 people swipe through the same stack of movies. Only the movies *everyone* said yes to make it through, and then the app draws tonight's pick or settles it with a head-to-head showdown.

## How it works
1. Add 2–6 people, pick a vibe (feel-good, scary, date night and more) and any filters you want. Or skip the catalogue and type in your own list of movies, one per line.
2. Everyone swipes the same movies: right for yes, left to skip, up for "must watch" (max 3 each). Tap a ticket to flip it for the director, cast, a trailer and where to stream it.
3. The results show what everyone said yes to and what almost made it, plus fun facts about the group.
4. Draw tonight's movie (must-watch picks get extra tickets), or settle it with a head-to-head showdown.

## Two ways to play
- **One phone:** pass it around. A hand-off screen makes sure nobody peeks at the others' answers.
- **Each on your own phone:** swipe, then send a link to the next person. When everyone is done, the results and the drawn movie show up on every phone by themselves. No account or download needed. If live updates can't connect, the last person sends the results back with a link instead.

## Also
- 570 movies from 27 countries, including 20 Norwegian films.
- Filters for genre, country, decade and length, and you can hide movies you've seen.
- History of past movie nights, and sharing of results.
- Can be installed on your phone and works offline.
- Keyboard controls and screen-reader labels.

## Privacy
No accounts and no tracking. Names, settings, history and your seen list stay in your browser. A share link holds the names and answers of that game, so only send it to the people playing. On separate phones, the same names and answers pass through [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh), a free message relay, in a channel with a random name that is deleted after about 12 hours.
